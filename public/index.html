Set up TheVoice

Five minutes now, and Claude will write in your audience’s own words.

Where is your audience?

Pick up to 3 places. Quotes come only from these locations. App Store reviews are country-level; a city or region helps find local Reddit communities and ranks quotes that mention it. Only English text is kept.

Your competitors (optional)

Add up to 5 apps your audience uses and we’ll read their recent App Store reviews in each country you picked above. You can skip this and track problems or communities instead.

Problems you want to find (optional)

What problem do you want to find in your users? Write it in your own words, for example “revenge trading” or “not following my system”. We search Reddit for people describing it, skip promotions, and highlight matching quotes everywhere. Up to 8.

Communities (optional)

Reddit communities where your audience talks. Up to 8.
